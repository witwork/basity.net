PUNCHCRAFT

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026 · Applies to the PunchCraft mobile app, published by Basity ("we", "us").

The short version: PunchCraft has no accounts, no sign-in, and collects no personal information. Everything you do in the game — every element you discover — stays on your device.

1. Data stored on your device only

The app stores the following on your device and nowhere else:

  • Your discovered elements, canvas layout, and game progress
  • Level, energy, and daily-streak state
  • Settings such as haptics and reminder preferences

This data is never uploaded, never shared, and is deleted when you delete the app. We have no way to access it.

2. On-device AI

On supported iPhones, combining two undiscovered words may generate a new element using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. This happens entirely on your device: no prompt, no result, and no gameplay data is ever sent to us or to any server.

3. Purchases

The optional PunchCraft Pro subscription is processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your name, payment details, or billing address.

4. No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The current version of PunchCraft contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking of any kind. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for any consent the law requires.

5. Notifications

If you enable the daily seed reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

6. Children

PunchCraft is not directed at children under 13, and since we collect no personal information, we also collect none from children.

7. Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions? Email support@basity.net.
