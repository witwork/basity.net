Common questions

My purchase didn't unlock Pro

Open Settings → Restore purchases inside the app. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PunchCraft Pro → Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Why can't two elements merge?

Elements marked with ✨ are final forms — they're the end of their branch and can't be combined further. Everything else is fair game: if a combo doesn't click, it just isn't discovered yet.

I ran out of energy

Energy refills automatically over time (1 every 2 minutes). Re-merging elements you've already discovered is always free. PunchCraft Pro removes the energy limit entirely.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device. If you deleted the app, Apple's app data was removed with it and we're unable to restore it — we don't keep copies of your data (see our Privacy Policy).

Report a bug or suggest an element

Email support@basity.net with a screenshot if you can. Element ideas are welcome — the funniest ones make it into updates.
