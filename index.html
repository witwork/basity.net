DARE & DRINK

Chill or reckless —
you set the dial.

Adult party drinking game for iPhone · 17+

Dare & Drink is the truth-or-drink party game where your group tunes the intensity question by question, from Chill (1) to Reckless (5). Every card calls players out by name. Works 100% offline. No account. No data collected.
