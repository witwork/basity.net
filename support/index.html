Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the paywall or Settings → Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, or Settings → Manage subscription inside the App.

Do I need an account?
No. Dare Dial has no accounts and works fully offline.

Where is my data stored?
Everything (player names, custom decks, settings) stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it all. See our Privacy Policy.

The party got interrupted — can we continue?
Yes. Reopen the app and tap "Resume party?" on the home screen.
